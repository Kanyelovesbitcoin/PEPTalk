# GlowPep Product Direction Bonus Slides

These slides follow the pre-launch UX review while shifting GlowPep toward a peptide-only tracking product.

## Slide 1: Trust Is A Feature

Peptides are a sensitive category. GlowPep should make transparency feel like part of the product, not legal copy bolted onto the bottom.

- Keep research-only and education-only framing visible.
- Keep legal-region labels readable for every user.
- Use research labels as orientation, not hype.
- Explain what stays local and what is sent to AI.
- Never paywall safety context, warnings, privacy controls, or data deletion.

## Slide 2: Rotation Is The Moat

The core loop is not a recommendation. It is a private record.

Library reference -> tracker setup -> Today's Rotation -> one-tap log or skip -> daily check-in -> exportable record.

- Free users get a useful daily queue.
- Pro users get private reminders, exports, and deeper guide context.
- The emotional reward is knowing what is due next without turning the app into a prescriber.
- This is more defensible than a static peptide catalog.

## Slide 3: Quiet Notifications

Notifications should feel like a discreet concierge, not a growth channel.

- Routine reminders should be generic on the lock screen.
- No compound name, amount, or sensitive detail.
- Reminders are a premium accountability layer.
- Do not request OS notification permission until the user explicitly enables a reminder.
- Future expansion: check-in recovery and rotation cadence.

## Slide 4: Your Record

Premium users should feel that GlowPep is building their private record, not trapping their data.

- Export shortlists, amount logs, check-ins, schedules, recipes, and saved AI context.
- JSON is the full backup format.
- CSV supports amount logs and daily check-ins.
- Schema v2 is peptide-only and contains no image fields.
- Data ownership makes a $20/month product feel more serious.

## Slide 5: Free vs Pro

Free should be useful enough to earn trust. Pro should be obvious once the user wants continuity.

| Free | Pro |
| --- | --- |
| Library browsing | Unlimited AI shortlist matching |
| Legal and safety labels | Full compound depth |
| One AI shortlist preview | Multiple saved shortlists |
| Today's Rotation | Private routine reminders |
| Basic local tracking | Full Your Record export |
| Privacy controls | Deeper AI guidance |

The Pro promise: your record, private reminders, progress over time, and deeper educational guidance.
